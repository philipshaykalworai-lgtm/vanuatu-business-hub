About Vanuatu Business Hub

Connecting Vanuatu businesses. Creating opportunities. Supporting local enterprise.

Who We Are

Vanuatu Business Hub is an online business marketplace designed to connect customers, businesses, suppliers and service providers throughout Vanuatu.

Our platform provides local businesses with an opportunity to introduce their products and services, increase their visibility and reach potential customers.

Our Mission

To make it easier for people to discover local businesses and help Vanuatu businesses grow through digital connections and opportunities.

Our Vision

To become a trusted online business directory and marketplace connecting communities and businesses across all six provinces of Vanuatu.

Connect

Bring customers and local businesses together.

Trade

Help people discover products and services available locally.

Grow

Give businesses greater visibility and opportunities.

Serving All Six Provinces

Torba · Sanma · Penama · Malampa · Shefa · Tafea

Our goal is to make business discovery easier throughout Vanuatu, from the northern islands to the southern provinces.
