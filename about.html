About Vanuatu Business Hub

Connecting Vanuatu businesses. Creating opportunities. Supporting local enterprise across all six provinces.

Who We Are

Vanuatu Business Hub is an online business marketplace designed to connect customers, businesses, suppliers and service providers throughout Vanuatu.

Our platform provides local businesses with an opportunity to introduce their products and services, increase their visibility and reach potential customers.

We aim to make it easier for people to discover local businesses and support the growth of enterprise in communities throughout Vanuatu.

Connecting Vanuatu

One platform bringing together customers, entrepreneurs, suppliers and service providers to create stronger business connections and opportunities.
